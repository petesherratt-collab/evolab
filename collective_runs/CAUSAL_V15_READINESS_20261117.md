# Causal 1.5 preparation complete

Prepared 17 November 2026. No paid model calls were made.

## Implemented corrections

- Restored class-specific combination, calibration and reliability instructions.
- Added original interpretation descriptions to purchased results and an active-
  item interpretation registry to purchased routing data. This discloses pair
  numbers, branch probes and costs without revealing unpurchased observations.
- Kept catalogue descriptions ambiguous and preserved causal worlds, costs and
  evidence budgets. The observed signature can now be reconstructed from the
  disclosed rules in the audited routed cases.
- Disclosed the two-tool-round schedule, current remaining rounds and batching
  capability to callers; made the final acquisition opportunity explicit.
- Replaced the blanket caution against candidate intersections with instructions
  distinguishing stated compatibility rules from independence assumptions.
- Added observations of tool availability, requested IDs, tool results (including
  denials) and stopping reasons. Retained scout handoff, bounded report recovery
  and future-participant reserves.

The strategy does not force the model to buy an oracle-selected route. The
scripted-client test demonstrates that a route can fit the existing schedule;
whether a live model executes it successfully remains to be measured.

## Validation

- Full regression suite: **76 passed, 80 subtests passed**, in 218.97 seconds.
- Mechanical/public-contract audit: **18/18 cases passed**, two seeds across all
  nine class/difficulty cells. The 20-record audit ledger verifies.
- All 12 routed audit cases passed the purchased-text interpretation replay,
  registry coverage, costs and absence-of-candidate-names checks.
- The executor test bought the router and then its active bundle in two tool
  rounds, within budget, before returning a report.
- Denial tests recorded unknown and over-budget requests without charging them.
- Existing causal-1.4 frozen source hashes remain unchanged.

The public replay identifies the observed signature; it does not claim that
agents receive the private world weights or can reproduce exact oracle
posteriors. The revised public disclosure also prevents treating cross-version
score changes as an isolated estimate of strategy improvement.
